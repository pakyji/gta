# About

**GTA Online Community** is a free community app for GTA Online players on PS4 and PS5. Players can chat live by platform, read GTA Online news and guides, and ask one of two AI assistants for help. The app is a Progressive Web App and also ships as an Android APK.

**What it includes**

- Platform chat rooms for PS4 and PS5
- Curated GTA Online news, with an optional live RSS feed
- Guides for money, outfits, glitches and GCTF, plus an index of working glitches
- A vehicle browser, a music player and a fun hub
- Two AI assistants (you bring your own API key, or use the free provider)

**Good to know**

- Not affiliated with Rockstar Games.
- Glitch entries are unverified. Patches change what works, so test before you rely on a method.
- Glitch titles and links come from gtaglitches.com and the r/gtaglitches community. Step-by-step text is not copied; follow the link to the source.

**Built with:** Next.js, React, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Drizzle ORM, Tailwind CSS, and a WebView Android shell.
